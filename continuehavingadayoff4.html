CONTINUE HAVING A DAY OFF

You feel a little better after the walk.

You are back home and it's time for a late lunch/early dinner.

You go to the kitchen, take the few ingredients you have left in the fridge, and you decide that it will be enough to cook a simple pasta dish.

You boil the water, put in the pasta and prepare the rest.

Suddenly you realize that you have been staring at the boiling pasta for the last 20 minutes and only thinking of how anxious and desperate you are;

how you are throwing a towel on this journey to New York and that the last 5 months would have been for nothing;

the things your friends and family will think and say because you didn't have the guts to push through it, to put your feelings aside for a moment and finish what you came here to do.

As you've totally overcooked the pasta, you are thinking of how to proceed after having had this inner dilemma resurface again:

  • Continue to repress the anxiety of the waste of time and at least try to have the day for yourself and to tell yourself that life is not all about the work especially at the expense of your sanity, that who cares what your friends will think because you need a break to not lose your mind and have a massive burnout, the real friends will understand; and as your family is concerned, they will understand, they have always understood.
  • Quickly eat the overcooked pasta, take all the stuff you need for working on your projects and run to the library because you don't want to be left at home with these thoughts, you need to work, you can't let your friends, your family and most importantly you can't let yourself down: you have to work, you have to be the best, you have to prove yourself and you have to succeed.
