CONTINUE HAVING A DAY OFF

You feel a little better after having finally done something for yourself, although not fully being able to enjoy it, but it's a start.

You think a little walk and fresh air would do you good. So you put on a hoodie and go outside.

It's a sunny day but not too hot: the perfect weather for a walk. You take out your headphones, put on your “feel good” playlist and head to the park.

Suddenly you realize that you have been just mechanically walking, without noticing numerous songs, which usually get you in a joyful mood, and you have already walked past the park and all you have done is think about how anxious and desperate you are:

how you are throwing a towel on this journey to New York and that the last 5 months would have been for nothing;

the things your friends and family will think and say because you didn't have the guts to push through it, to put your feelings aside for a moment and finish what you came here to do.

As you've spent almost an hour walking but seem to cannot enjoy it, you are thinking of how to proceed after having had this inner dilemma resurface again:

  • Continue to repress the anxiety of the waste of time and at least try to have the day for yourself and to tell yourself that life is not all about the work especially at the expense of your sanity, that who cares what your friends will think because you need a break to not lose your mind and have a massive burnout, the real friends will understand; and as your family is concerned, they will understand, they have always understood.
  • Turn around, quickly walk back home, take all the stuff you need for working on your projects and run to the library because you don't want to be left at home with these thoughts, you need to work, you can't let your friends, your family and most importantly you can't let yourself down: you have to work, you have to be the best, you have to prove yourself and you have to succeed.
