GO TO BED

The next morning you wake up, having had a sort of okay sleep, but you are still tired; you realize that all the accumulated anxieties and sleep deprivation could not be solved in one night and immediately think of the loss of time of yesterday's evening.

You should have known that one night of only fine and not even great sleep would not change a thing and instead of actually spending time to do something you would enjoy (watching a movie or going out with your friends) or actually finishingdevising a plan for today, you have done neither of those things.

You feel frustrated and your choice to go to bed yesterday has instead of relieving the pressure of time only intensified the pressure.

In the midst of all this frustration you see 2 ways of how to go on with your day:

  • Because you feel so tired and irritated, you perceive yesterday's choice as a sign that you need to take a day off and spend the day not doing anything that is related to your projects, have the day totally for yourself.
  • Because you feel so irritated and frustrated of yourself that you did not think of today yesterday, all you could see yourself doing is to forget it and go the library as soon as possible to not lose any more of your time that you should have been your priority yesterday.
