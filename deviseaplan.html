The anxiety from the internal pressures is growing and what appeared to you beforehand like an anxiety-releaving plan has turned out to be an anxiety-enducing use of your time.

So as you're struggling to write the plan for tomorrow, you are thinking of how to proceed after having had this inner dilemma resurface again:

  • You think that going to bed right now and having as much sleep as possible tonight would help you the most, would be the most efficient and productive way how to spend your time tonight in order to be ready for tomorrow, so you leave everything as it is and go to bed.
  • You think that tomorrow you will feel better about yourself if you just push through this surge of anxiety, finish writing the to-do list for tomorrow, because you need to be prepared, this is the time to show what you're capable of, and being on top of things is the answer on how to succeed.
