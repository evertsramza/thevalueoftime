WATCH A MOVIE

You realize that you don't remember the last time you watched a movie just for yourself, to just have fun, to not immediately think about the connections that your professors thought of when assigning it for the class to watch.

You think of a movie that you would enjoy, the first that comes to your mind is Jurassic Park.

You've seen it a million times but that is exactly what makes it the perfect movie, you know the plot, you know how it would make you feel, you get excited. So you put on Jurassic Park.

Twenty minutes in you start fidget in your couch, start moving around, changing positions and after 10 more minutes of moving around you realize that you haven't really been enjoying the film and instead been thinking about your projects, how stressed you are about how they will turn out and all the details that you want to and still need to work on.

As soon as the thought of not enjoying a film enters your mind, it is accompanied by the feeling of frustration that instead of resting and going to bed early so that you would feel refreshed and ready to have a productive next day working on your projects, you've chosen to watch a 2-hour long movie.

And as much as you try to tell yourself that you had a nice time, that you finally did something for yourself to take the mind off the work and how essential it was for your well-being, you cannot let go of the thought that tomorrow you will look back on your choices and feel irritated with yourself, because you don't feel rested, you don't seem to have gotten the worry-free enjoyment which was the very reason why you chose to watch a movie in the first place.

As you've spent half an hour “watching” the film, you are thinking of how to proceed after having had this inner dilemma resurface again:

  • Continue watching the movie because you need to take your mind off your projects, off the pressures of succeeding, you need to have a break and finally do something for yourself, to do something that makes you happy.
  • Turn off the movie because: what were you thinking? You've wasted half-an-hour of your valuable time, that should have been spent either making a provisional plan for tomorrow or going to bed early.
